🎮 Steam Games — Market & Player Engagement Dashboard

Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Steam games across market trends, player engagement, pricing, reviews, genres, and release trends.

- Total games
- Active vs free vs paid games
- Top 10 games by Peak CCU
- Paid vs free game distribution

- Top genres by average Peak CCU
- Average Peak CCU: Free vs Paid games

3. Pricing & Player Engagement
- Distribution of Steam game prices

- Top genres by average user score

🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze Steam game data and identify patterns in player engagement, pricing, popularity, reviews, genres, and release trends.
